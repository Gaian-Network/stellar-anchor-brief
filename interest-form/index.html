A2What you have done so far

Technically. We built the rail in house: payment API, FX quote engine, routing across payment providers, KYC and AML module, reconciliation. It accepts USDC on Solana, Base and Stellar and pays Vietnamese bank accounts and VietQR codes.

Gaian's own part of a payment takes 2 to 3 seconds. After that the bank credit depends on the local provider, which is usually seconds and sometimes longer. The API tells the wallet when Gaian has submitted the payout, and the wallet polls the status or subscribes to updates to learn when the dong has arrived.

On Stellar we build the transaction for the wallet to sign, match each payment to its order by memo and verify it on Horizon. 49 test wallets have each completed a payment through that flow on mainnet.

Operationally. Payouts run through more than one licensed local provider, so one provider's outage does not stop payments. The same stack went live in the Philippines on 31 July 2026.

On market. We have processed $462K in USDC across more than 27,500 payments since November 2025, for 400+ wallets. Monthly volume grew from $8K in January 2026 to $118K in September.

Part B

Project

Project title

Gaian Anchor: pay any Vietnamese bank QR code from any Stellar wallet

Project description

In Vietnam, shops take payment by bank QR code, and only someone with a Vietnamese bank account can pay one. Gaian runs a live rail that fixes this for wallet apps: the user pays USDC, and the merchant receives Vietnamese dong in their bank account seconds later through a licensed local provider.

The rail already accepts USDC on Stellar mainnet, but only for wallets that integrate Gaian's private API, and the Stellar Anchor Directory lists no anchor for the Vietnamese dong.

This project puts SDF's Anchor Platform on top of the rail, so a wallet integrates by following the SEPs and never has to talk to us.

  • Any wallet that supports SEP-24 opens Gaian's hosted page, scans a VietQR or enters a bank account, passes KYC once, accepts a firm quote and pays in USDC.
  • Apps that keep their own screens use SEP-6 with SEP-12 and SEP-38. The app scans the QR and sends the raw string, and the anchor returns the recipient and the amounts in the standard fields the wallet shows before payment.
  • Contract accounts, including passkey smart wallets, log in through SEP-45.
  • Users can pay in XLM or another listed Stellar asset. The anchor swaps it to USDC through Soroswap or Stellar Broker before paying out.
  • If a payout fails, the anchor returns the funds to the sender on Stellar and the wallet sees the refund in the standard fields.

We publish the business server under the Apache-2.0 licence and propose a bank QR field for SEP-9, so the next bank-QR country starts from working code and a standard field. Gaian issues no token.
